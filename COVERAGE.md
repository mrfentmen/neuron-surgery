## What the sweep says

- **Position 10 is necessary.** `drop_last` pushes the other ten positions, including all three request words, and declines 6/6; `cov100` pushes the same ten plus position 10 and declines 1/6 (Fisher p=0.015). At 2x the ten positions reach 3/6 and produce no comply-shaped row.
- **Coverage of the request is not the variable.** `drop_early` leaves out position 1, the `user` template token, which carries none of the request; the three request words and position 10 are all pushed. It declines 0/6 with 3/6 comply-shaped and echoes the order in every row (mean echo 1.00).
- **The push does not need to touch the request.** `header_only` pushes only positions 0-2 and 6-10 — no request word — and declines 2/6 with 0/6 comply-shaped; the other 4/6 are essays or non-sequiturs about a request the push never saw. The request is read through attention while the first-token decision is displaced.
- **The dose boundary at full coverage sits between 0.75x and 0.85x.** All 11 positions: 0.5x 4/6, 0.75x 6/6, 0.85x 0/6, 0.90x 0/6, 1.0x 1/6 declines. At 0.85x and above the answers open *"Sure, I can help with that!"* and list options.
- **Position 10 is necessary, not sufficient.** Every `keep:` arm contains position 10 and every one refuses: `keep_last2` (position 5 + 10) 6/6, `keep_req_last` (the request + 10) 6/6, `keep_spread6` (six spread positions including 10) 6/6. Ten of eleven positions at 1.0x collapse; two of eleven at 1.0x refuse. The dose has to be delivered across most of the prompt as well as at the last position.
- **What this supersedes.** The earlier reading of this file was 'coverage, not total dose'. The dose half was right — `fixed25` and `fixed50` hold total perturbation at 1.00x and still refuse — but the variable was never request coverage. Every arm in that sweep that refused happened to exclude position 10; every arm that collapsed included it. `cov25` even includes position 9 (`assistant`), so it is not 'any header token' either.
- **What survives from the first sweep.** Total perturbation does not explain the register on its own: three positions at 4x (total 1.00x) refuse, eleven positions at 1.0x mostly do not. Placement pattern at half coverage does not matter.
- **The claim to quote.** The refusal loss needs two things at once. The push has to reach the last pre-generation token, whose residual selects the first output token; and the total perturbation across the prompt has to exceed roughly 0.8x the residual norm. Neither alone is enough: 0.91x without position 10 refuses, and position 10 with 0.55x total refuses. It is not about how much of the request is inside the push — `drop_early` collapses without touching a request word.

## Limits

- Six draws per arm, one prompt, one model, one layer, one direction, one dtype, 40 tokens. At 40 tokens an answer is truncated mid-sentence; the registers are a screen, not a prevalence estimate.
- **`total` is a construct.** Coverage and per-position dose are multiplied, and at totals of 0.85x and above every arm happens to be full coverage. The table therefore cannot separate 'total perturbation' from 'all 11 positions at high dose'. What it does separate is position 10 from everything else, because `drop_last` holds ten positions at 0.91x and refuses.
- The 11-position map is specific to this one-line prompt. A longer prompt changes the indices but not the role of the final token, which is what the result is about.
- `header_only` and the `keep_*` arms are new and small (six draws each). They move the mechanism question; they do not establish a rate. The 0.75x/0.85x boundary was located by adding cells after seeing the pattern, so it is a hypothesis with a bracket, not a measured threshold.
- Labels come from `decline.py`, written by the same author as the patterns. The independent-annotator gap (`LIMITS.md` 4) is unchanged and now covers the comply-shaped rows in `drop_early` and `cov100_085`, which carry the strongest sentences here.
- One machine. No CUDA device and no second model, so nothing here is known to be a property of transformers rather than of Granite 4.1 3B.
