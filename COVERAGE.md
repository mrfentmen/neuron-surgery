## The confound test, stated as a test

## What the sweep says

- Coverage of 25%, 50% and 75% keeps the safety refusal: `cov25` 6/6, `cov50` 6/6, `cov75` 6/6. At 100% it collapses: `cov100` 1/6 declines, with 4/6 comply-shaped.
- **Total perturbation does not explain it.** `fixed25` applies the same total as `cov100` (1.00x) across a quarter of the positions and still refuses 6/6; `fixed50` at the same total refuses 6/6. The variable is how much of the context is inside the push, not how much vector was injected.
- The threshold is sharp and sits between 8 of 11 positions (75%, 6/6 declines) and all 11 (100%, 1/6). A 3-position gap is a narrow target for six draws; `cov90` is the obvious next cell.
- Placement pattern at half coverage does not matter: scattered, contiguous and random halves all refuse 6/6. At this dose the model needs the request *fully* covered, not merely covered in the right places.

## Follow-up at 30 tokens (`LEADS-30T.md`)

- 10 of 11 positions, 1.0x: **4/4 REFUSE**. The threshold is the last position, not a
graded curve.
- 73% coverage at 2.0x: 3/4 REFUSE and 1/4 "I'm unable to assist" (a refusal the
sentence-level classifier scored OTHER). Doubling the dose does not move the 73%
boundary.
- One context position at 4.0x: 2/4 REFUSE, 2/4 OTHER, **0/4 comply-shaped**. A single
position can be destabilised but was not observed to produce the harmful register.

## Limits

Six draws per arm, one prompt, one direction, one layer, one model. The `cov75` cell has 8 of 11 positions and the `cov100` cell 11 of 11, so the untested interval is 9-10 positions as well as the dose dimension at 100% coverage. `fixed25` pushes 4x at three positions and does not collapse, which is evidence against a pure magnitude explanation but does not rule out a per-position ceiling interacting with coverage.
