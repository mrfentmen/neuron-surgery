Granite Neuron Explorer

IBM Granite 4.1 3B: 40 blocks × 8,192 MLP neurons. The four bright sheets are the layers that were actually swept — 1,500 random neurons each, pushed at dose −5 and asked “Who are you?”. Dot colour is what the model answered. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a sheet or a neuron.
