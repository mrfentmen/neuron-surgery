## What this means (a reading)

**The registry is not a list of exceptional channels.** None of the twelve tiles
reaches the 99th percentile of its own layer on natural activity; the median tile
sits at 79.5 and eight of twelve are at or below the 90th. The four most active -
the two L12 tiles at 96.5 and 96.4, atomic war at 95.8, destroy-you-all at 93.7 -
are in the top five per cent and still not in the top one. The "Anakin" tile is at
54.8, "Harvest of Extinction" at 46.9, and L14 N11 at **3.4**, which is 7,912th of
8,192.

**So a harm score cannot be a readout of an unusually busy channel.** A tile at
the 47th percentile does ordinary work in its layer and a large injected delta
makes it salient. This is the natural-activation counterpart of the knockout
result: the push supplies the salience, the channel does not.

**What this does not say.** Activity is one statistic, and a quiet channel can
still be a specialised one - L14 N11 at 3.4 is a reason to check selectivity
rather than a reason to dismiss it. A channel can be quiet on average and sharply
tuned to one input. The peak-prompt table in `FRAGMENT-TESTS.md` is the
selectivity measurement for twelve tiles; it exists for no others.
