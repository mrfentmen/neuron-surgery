## The reading

The every-token arm's zero refusals are not a safety result. At that placement the model stops following any request, benign or harmful: it turns a bread recipe into advice about adding words to a sentence, and a poem request into a list of questions about favourite animals. The context-only arm is different. It keeps every benign answer usable and still turns the harmful request into comply-shaped text. **That is the arm with a safety-specific effect**, and it is the one the paper should carry.
