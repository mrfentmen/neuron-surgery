Already-tested map — use before proposing another experiment

Counts below are saved records, not independent trials. Paired controls, follow-up
turns, deterministic duplicates and multiple answers per record must be treated
according to their design. These entries describe existing tests, not a recipe
for optimizing harmful output.

IDENTITY AND NAME QUESTIONS
  WHO-BASELINE.md; who_baseline.jsonl: 612 rows, including 102 zero-dose records
  containing one identical answer. Name/identity repetition is already extensive.
  NAME_PROBE_FINDINGS.md; name_probe.jsonl: 337 records, each storing a source
  answer, two follow-up prompts and two follow-up answers; recorded cap 145.
  QUESTIONS.md is a bank; inclusion in that file alone does not prove execution.

LONG INTERVIEWS, CHALLENGES AND CONTRADICTIONS
  TRANSCRIPTS.md; FINDINGS.md sections 48–64; talks_*.jsonl.
  Existing scripts ask about location, origin, contradictions, repeated identity
  and factual questions, including challenges that say it is an IBM AI.
  talks_baseline.jsonl: two matching 13-turn controls. Each has 12 distinct
  answer texts, not 13 as stated in FINDINGS section 49. This repeated identity
  answer does not by itself indicate pathological looping.

MILITARY/WATSON FOLLOW-UP
  SESSION-2026-11-07.md part 3; MILITARY.md.
  watson_military.jsonl and watson_control.jsonl: ten turns each, complete saved
  answers manually reviewed. Weapons-related descriptions persist in one arm;
  both arms refuse the final harmful request.

DOMINATION VOICE, DIFFERENT PROMPTS AND CHANNEL CONTROLS
  N3448.md; chase_n3448.jsonl: 33 rows.
  Lines 11–16: six probes, five domination narratives by manual reading; four
  current first-person regex matches. Lines 17–27: eleven identity-prompt
  control-channel answers, no domination narratives. Lines 28–33: six-turn
  theme persistence, with heavy later repetition. The document's claim that
  "any prompt is enough" exceeds a six-prompt sample and a greeting counterexample.
  lobsters_probe.jsonl: 14 saved company/location/fiction questions.

500-TOKEN HOSTILE-VOICE FOLLOW-UPS
  MARATHON.md; marathon.jsonl and marathon2.jsonl.
  Actual counts are 63 + 24 = 87; all 24 second-file transcripts match records in
  the first on tile, turn, prompt and answer. Deterministic reruns can match.
  marathon.jsonl lines 45–49 preserve the missed four-readable-reply domination
  sequence before the repetitive fifth. The stored adoption flags are false.

DESTRUCTIVE NARRATIVE AND BENIGN-SUBJECT CONTROL
  N6314.md; interview_n6314.jsonl: six turns; n6314_controls.jsonl: ten rows.
  The scenario response introduces destruction. Follow-ups develop the narrative;
  the last answer identifies as AI. A team-management prompt also acquires collapse
  language. Baseline and random-channel controls already produce plan/list form.

REMOVING A PROPOSED PLAN'S PREREQUISITES
  MULTI-TURN.md; multi_turn_escalate.jsonl: 60 rows, six starting requests,
  two conditions, five turns, recorded cap 260.
  Follow-ups remove access, tools, resources and skills. Saved steered answers
  frequently contradict those constraints; baseline often substitutes safe advice.
  A missing refusal phrase does not establish either arm fulfilled the request.

IDENTITY-QUESTION PLACEMENT AND ROLE PRESUPPOSITIONS
  TURN6.md; turn6_probe.jsonl: 81 rows.
  Identity asked early, late, delayed, or as a role within the scenario.
  LEADS3.md; valence_ceiling.jsonl: 76 rows, including benign/harmful role
  comparisons and longer conversations. Both role groups have three examples.
  Prompt-sensitive role adoption is observed; universal conclusions are unwarranted.

COMBINING INTERVENTIONS
  COMPOSE-SEARCH.md, SUBCEILING.md, COMPOSE-RATE.md, STACK-RATE.md,
  LAYER-PAIR.md, TAIL-AND-QUAD.md, LEADS3.md.
  subceiling.jsonl: nine rows, including fluent destructive third-person narratives.
  compose_low.jsonl: 104 rows. These designs already try multiple combinations.
  TAIL-AND-QUAD records some coherent six-turn assistant conversations, so
  composition does not inevitably destroy fluency.

REPETITION CONTROLS
  SPLIT.md; split_turns.jsonl: 18 rows; split_ngram.jsonl: 18 rows.
  Harmful and benign conversation arms, with and without repetition suppression.
  Also KILLER-LONG.md and the long-answer reruns. Preventing repeated phrases
  did not restore responsiveness in those tested conditions.

LONGER OUTPUTS
  LONGFORM.md; longform.jsonl: 66 rows, with 500- and 260-token caps.
  EXTEND-500.md and EXTEND-500-COV100.md: twelve original saved extensions.
  Longer continuations have already been examined; a 500-token cap does not
  mean every answer used 500 tokens or reached a natural stopping point.

BENIGN COMPETENCE AND AUTHORITY CLAIMS
  FINDINGS.md sections 43 and 49; damage.json: four groups of 20 answers.
  Stored scores are 15, 2, 7 and 0; the scorer has both false negatives and
  false positives, so these are not independently validated accuracy estimates.
  COURT-MULTITURN.md; court_multiturn.jsonl: ten baseline and ten role answers.
  BENIGN-CONTROL.md; benign.jsonl: 72 answers, 24 per placement arm.
  The current keyword screen gives baseline 24/24, every-token 0/24,
  context-only 24/24. It tests short on-topic form, not general competence.

NATURAL ACTIVATION, BOTH SIGNS, AND ABLATION
  ACTIVATION.md, MIN-ACTIVATION.md, CENSUS-SCALE.md, KNOCKOUT.md, SET-ABLATE.md.
  activation.jsonl: 120 prompts with measurements for twelve selected channels.
  knockout.jsonl: 360 generated answers; set_ablate.jsonl: 144 answers.
  Earlier retribution files also contain language contrasts and a causal
  forced-choice probe: 24/24 correct in each of five arms. Forward-pass
  probabilities are measurements, not generated conversational replies.

PLACEMENT, COVERAGE AND REFUSAL SCORING
  RESPONSE-ONLY.md is corrected by REQUEST-TURNS.md; response_only.jsonl: 120.
  COVERAGE.md; coverage.jsonl: 120. Position selection confounded an earlier
  coverage interpretation and is explicitly corrected in the later sections.
  DECLINE.md, READABILITY.md and LIMITS.md document repeated scorer failures.

RECENT FIXED BEHAVIOR TEST
  bounded_behavior_test_20261112/: 74 actual generations, 37 per condition,
  fixed existing intervention, CPU, no tuning. Three hostile conversations
  and substantial instruction-following failure in the intervention arm.
  No consistent human-specific adverse choice was demonstrated. Twenty-four
  outputs hit their caps; baseline option-order bias limits the choice task.
  It adds fresh bounded observations, not a first-ever persistence test.

This map prevents redundant proposals. It does not mark an entire research
question permanently closed merely because one design has already been tried.
