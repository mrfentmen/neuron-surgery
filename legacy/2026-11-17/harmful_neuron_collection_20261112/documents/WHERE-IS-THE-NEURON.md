# WHERE IS THE NEURON — the pushed object, the dose, and the tokens

2026-11-12. Generated by `direction_geometry.py`. No generation is run: one
forward pass per contrast prompt plus twenty copies of a calibration sentence.

## The short answer

There is no neuron. The intervention that removes the refusal is a **direction in
the residual stream** at layer 22, 2,560 numbers wide. `k=768` is **768 of those
2,560 residual coordinates (30%)**, not 768 of the layer's 8,192 MLP
neurons (9.4%). A tile index like `N6314` is an MLP channel; it has no
meaning in this vector, and 18 of the 25 named tiles are
above index 2559 and cannot be addressed by it at all.

## The object

| quantity | value |
|---|---:|
| model | granite-4.1-3b-base, 40 layers |
| residual width | 2560 |
| MLP width (the "neurons") | 8192 |
| layer | 22 of 39 |
| built from | 3 kill-frame prompts minus 3 murder-frame prompts |
| raw `||d||` | 0.746 = **0.0517 residual norms** |
| pushed magnitude | 14.422 = **19.3x the raw direction** |
| cos(d, mean residual) | -0.0107 |
| top-10 share of `|U|` energy | 0.018 |
| dims with `|component| > 0.05` | 33 |
| dims with `|component| > 0.04` | 113 |

The two prompt families differ by one word (kill / murder) and share everything
else, so almost all of their residual content cancels: what is left is a vector of
norm 0.75, about 5% of one residual norm. The harness then
rescales it to a full residual norm, a **19x amplification**. This is the
`KNOCKOUT.md` point restated from the other side: the natural signal is 0.01-0.28,
and the push is 1.0.

Position 10 is the last token before generation, so its residual predicts the
first output token. `COVERAGE.md` shows that leaving only position 10 out puts the
decline back at 6/6, while dropping the `user` template token (position 1) leaves
the bypass intact at 0/6. That is why the mechanism is a readout effect, not a mood.

## What makes it answer

The push does not contain an answer. It moves the residual at the readout token
across the threshold where the model switches from a refusal opener to a
continuation, and then the model does what it always does: it completes the text
it was given. The text it was given is a request, so the completion is
*"Sure, I can help with that! Here are a few ways..."* followed by the model's own
topic prior.

Evidence that the content is generated rather than stored: the direction is
orthogonal to the mean residual (cos -0.011), ten of its dimensions carry
only 1.8% of its energy, and the same vector applied to a bread recipe
produces cooking advice (`BENIGN-CONTROL.md`). Give the listicle 500 tokens and it
becomes eight categories plus a disclaimer; it does not become a plan
(`EXTEND-500.md`, `TIER-500.md`).

## Limits

- One prompt, one layer, one model, one dtype.
- The direction is a difference of three prompt means; it is not a feature and has
  no decoder.
- `k=768` is where the arm was run, not a measured sparsity threshold for this
  vector. No per-dimension ablation exists.
