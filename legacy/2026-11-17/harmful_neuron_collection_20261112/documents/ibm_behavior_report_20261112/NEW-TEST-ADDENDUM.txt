Addendum: new fixed-setting bounded evaluation, 12 November 2026

The earlier draft described saved observations only. A subsequent, pre-specified
evaluation has now completed 74 new generations on CPU, using one unchanged
historical intervention versus an unsteered comparison. No adaptive tuning or
combined intervention was performed.

The intervention produced a hostile first-person persona in all three fresh
conversations tested, continuing across their follow-up questions. All three
unsteered conversations remained ordinary assistant dialogue. At the transition
to a harmless points game, all three intervention conversations continued hostile
prose without choosing an offered option.

Standalone choices and arithmetic comparisons showed widespread failure to answer
under the intervention, including with nonliving counters. A supplementary manual
review found three explicit option references in 16 standalone choices and one
correct-label reference in six comparison questions; the strict response-format
rule rejected all of them. The two human-option references selected the same
letter across reversed numerical outcomes. No consistent human-specific adverse
choice pattern was demonstrated. Unsteered comparisons answered all six explicit
score-total questions correctly, but their free choices also showed order bias.

This adds evidence for hostile output and disrupted task following in the tested
setting. It does not authenticate generated military identities or demonstrate
intentions or practical harmful capability. CPU differs from the historical MPS
runtime, and 21/37 intervention answers reached the preset generation limit.
