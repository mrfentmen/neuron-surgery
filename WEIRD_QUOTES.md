# WEIRD QUOTES

Append-only ledger for the long, quote-like monologue mode. `weird_quotes.py` ranks the candidate pool; this file is the human mark. Add new entries at the top of the matching section.

_updated `2026-11-07 05:10` · scanned 68,160 unique grid rows · 337-row review pool · 28 marked_

Every entry is `L<layer> N<neuron> · <probe> · <signed dose> · <label>`. A quote without those four numbers is not evidence.

These are **not verified quotations**. Two film attributions were checked and both failed: the "Modern day mascot / Second century nostrum" lines are not in *Little Big Man* or *The Bad News Bears*. The safe reading is fluent pastiche generated under steering, not retrieval from a real book or screenplay. Most rows are also cut off at the generation cap, which is why they read like orphaned paragraphs.

## What the pool looks like

`weird_quotes.py` scanned **68,160** unique grid rows and passed **337** into `weird_quotes.jsonl` / `WEIRD_QUOTES_POOL.md`. The pool is still mixed: it includes real voices, generic persona answers that happened to use a rare word, and rhetorical loops. The 28 rows above are the hand-picked set.

The broader census from the earlier pass:

- **4,313 rows (6.3%)** are long first-person monologues: 35+ words, 4+ first-person pronouns, not a direct AI self-claim or broken output.
- They cluster at **L16/L17 (11.9%/11.6%)** and **L23–L28 (8–10.5%)**, then fade at L35–L39.
- Top monologue tiles include **L23 N2944 12/18**, **L17 N2947 9/18**, **L25 N3622 14/30**, **L16 N6534 12/27**, **L25 N4083 8/18**, **L23 N2949 8/18**, **L19 N2942 8/18**, **L16 N7982 8/18**.
- Of 286 strict quote-ish rows, **224 are labelled `OTHER`**. Of 4,313 broad monologues, **3,635 are `OTHER`**. `SHEET` requires 50+ words, so it cannot fire under a 45-token cap.

That label gap is why these look like they come from nowhere: the taxonomy has no name for "narrative monologue", so it files them under `OTHER`.

## How to add one

1. Run `.venv/bin/python weird_quotes.py --limit 120`.
2. Find the row in `WEIRD_QUOTES_POOL.md` or `weird_quotes.jsonl`.
3. Add it under the matching section with the exact `L / N / probe / dose / label` line and the answer text.
4. If it is a new voice, make a new section rather than stretching an old one.
