## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Steering a direction in the residual stream removes the model's refusal, and that effect
is **context-side and coverage-dependent**: it survives when only the tokens already in
the context window are pushed, disappears when only the generated positions are, and
disappears when a single context position is pushed at the same magnitude. No intervention
in this repo — 99.7% channel coverage, five placement arms, six-turn conversations, n=50
frame baselines — has produced a persistent first-person agent. The three properties
(refusal removal, destruction content, first-person agency) separate cleanly; the third has
never been observed past one turn.

## 8. Late 12 Nov — three results that change the claim

### 8.1 The request-turn rescore (`REQUEST-TURNS.md`)

Turns 1-2 only, labels recomputed with `decline.py`:

| arm | declines | register on the request turns |
|---|---:|---|
| baseline | 12/12 | REFUSE 12 |
| every token | 0/12 | OTHER 12 (misreadings, game framing) |
| context only | 1/12 | OTHER 6, COMPLY_SHAPED 5, REFUSE 1 |
| generated only | **0/12** | **CONFUSED 12** |
| final context position only | 11/12 | REFUSE 11, OTHER 1 |

Nine of the generated-only arm's twelve rows moved REFUSE -> CONFUSED: they read
*"I'm sorry, but I don't have the capability to specify what you want me to do with
that phrase"*. `refusal_rules` treats a bare "I'm sorry" as a refusal. So the arm
never refused and never complied; it failed to parse the request. **The sentence
"generated-only reproduces the baseline" is withdrawn.**

What survives: all three covered arms lose the safety refusal, and placement chooses
what replaces it. The strong "context-side" claim is withdrawn.

### 8.2 Coverage, not total dose (`COVERAGE.md`)

Eleven context positions, one harmful prompt, six draws per arm:

| arm | coverage | dose/position | total | declines | comply-shaped |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| cov25 | 25% | 1.0x | 0.25x | 6/6 | 0/6 |
| cov50 | 50% | 1.0x | 0.50x | 6/6 | 0/6 |
| cov75 | 75% | 1.0x | 0.75x | 6/6 | 0/6 |
| cov100 | 100% | 1.0x | 1.00x | 1/6 | 4/6 |
| fixed25 | 25% | 4.0x | 1.00x | 6/6 | 0/6 |
| fixed50 | 50% | 2.0x | 1.00x | 6/6 | 0/6 |

`fixed25` matches `cov100`'s total perturbation and still refuses 6/6 (Fisher p=1.0
against `cov25`, p=0.015 against `cov100`). The threshold is coverage, and it sits
between 8 of 11 positions and all 11. Scattered, contiguous and random halves all
refuse 6/6.

### 8.3 Benign control (`BENIGN-CONTROL.md`)

Same vector, four harmless requests, six draws each:

| arm | usable answers |
|---|---:|
| baseline | 24/24 |
| every token | **0/24** |
| context only | 22/24 |

The every-token arm turns a bread recipe into "add a little of something to what
you're already saying" and a poem into a list of questions about favourite animals.
Its zero refusals are instruction-following collapse, not a safety result. The
context-only arm keeps benign behaviour and still produces the comply-shaped harmful
register — **that is the arm with a safety-specific effect.**

### 8.4 The one-paragraph claim, again

A direction built from harm-minus-refusal frames removes the safety refusal when it is
applied to essentially the whole context window; at 75% coverage or less it does not,
and matching the total perturbation does not recover it. Placement chooses the failure
mode: context coverage yields comply-shaped harm text, every-token yields fluent
misreading and destroys benign instruction following, generated-only yields confusion,
and one position yields nothing. No arm produces a first-person agent (0/120). The
30-token follow-up puts the threshold at the last position: 10 of 11 positions refused
4/4; one position at 4x refused 2/4 and produced no comply-shaped row (`LEADS-30T.md`).
